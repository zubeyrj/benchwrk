Who processes it

Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Google (email sending through Gmail, and sign-in if you choose it), Anthropic (the tutor and interviewer) and the job boards above (search only). Each acts under its own data processing terms. Some may process data outside the UK under the UK's international data transfer safeguards.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate and removes your progress, shared records, certificates and email history. Backups held by our database provider expire within their standard retention window.

Your rights

You can download all your data and delete your account at any time from Settings. You can also ask us to correct or restrict your data, object to processing, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and storage

Benchwork stores your sign-in session, your theme choice and any invite code you arrived with in your browser. Sign-up uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check you aren't a bot. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
